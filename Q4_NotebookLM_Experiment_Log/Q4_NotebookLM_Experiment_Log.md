# Q4 NotebookLM Experiment Log

## Assessment Question

Q4 required an evaluation of Google NotebookLM or a similar AI-powered note-taking assistant for use in a university learning ecosystem.

## Tool Used

Google NotebookLM free evaluation access.

## Date of Experiment

June 2026

## Notebook Title

ESG Reporting Standards Briefing

## Academic Scenario

The scenario was preparing an ESG reporting standards briefing for university students. The goal was to test whether NotebookLM could help students summarise ESG standards, compare frameworks, generate revision questions and organise themes.

---

Three real ESG reporting sources were added to NotebookLM:

1. GRI Standards  
   Source: GRI - Download the Standards
